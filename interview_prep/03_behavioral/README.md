# Behavioral Interview Hub

> Behavioral is make-or-break at Amazon. Every round has an LP question. A Bar Raiser will dig 3-4 follow-up questions deep. Prepare your stories so well you could deliver them half-asleep.

## STAR Format

```
S — Situation:  2-3 sentences. Set the scene. What was the context?
T — Task:       1-2 sentences. What were YOU responsible for? ("I was tasked with...")
A — Action:     3-5 sentences. What did YOU specifically do? (say "I", not "we")
R — Result:     1-2 sentences. What happened? MUST include a number or metric.
```

**Rules:**
- Say "I" — the interviewer is evaluating you, not your team.
- Every result must have a number. "Improved performance" → "-60% processing latency."
- Target < 2 minutes per story. If you're rambling past 2 min, cut the Situation.
- Prepare 15-20 stories total; rotate per company/LP.

## Why Behavioral Is Make-or-Break at Amazon

Amazon is different from every other company:
- **Every round has an LP question** — even the technical rounds open with "tell me about a time..."
- **The Bar Raiser is looking to downvote** — they probe until they find a weakness.
- **Follow-ups go 3-4 levels deep:** "Why did you do that?" → "What would you do differently?" → "What was the data behind that decision?" → "How did you handle the pushback?"
- **LP mismatch = rejection** — a perfect technical performance with weak LP signals does not pass.

---

## Rehearsal Protocol

1. **Daily (15 min, Phase 3 = weeks 9-12):** Pick one story. Deliver it out loud, timer running. No notes.
2. **Record yourself once per week:** Play it back. Cut anything that isn't Situation, Task, Action, or Result.
3. **"The metric check":** After each story, ask "did I say a number?" If no → it's not a complete story.
4. **Stress-test follow-ups:** Have a friend or use AI to ask "why did you choose that approach?" and "what went wrong?" after each story. Practice the depth, not just the surface.
5. **From week 10:** Do full 45-min mock behavioral interviews (Pramp, interviewing.io, or a friend).
